Our Story

Three generations around the same hearth

When Arthur Bellamy opened our doors on Kensington Row, he promised his guests a proper meal: honest ingredients, cooked with care, served without fuss.

Sixty years on, his granddaughter keeps that promise. Our menu still follows the farms and fishermen of the West Country, changing with each season.
